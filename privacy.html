Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Mrk Clips (“the app”) is a personal tool operated by its owner. This policy explains what information it handles.

What the app accesses

When the owner connects TikTok, the app receives an access token and a refresh token through TikTok Login Kit, plus basic profile information (user.info.basic). It uses the video.publish permission only to upload videos the owner created to the owner’s own account.

How information is stored

Tokens are stored only in a file on the owner’s own computer. They are not sent to any server operated by the app, and the app has no database, analytics, or advertising.

Sharing

The app does not sell, share, or disclose any information to third parties, other than sending videos to TikTok at the owner’s request.

Retention and deletion

The owner can revoke the app’s access at any time in TikTok’s settings (Security and permissions, then Manage apps) and delete the token file from the computer.
