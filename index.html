Mrk Clips

Mrk Clips is a personal tool that watches my livestreams, clips the best moments, adds captions, and uploads the finished videos to my own TikTok account.

How it works

  1. The tool records a rolling buffer of a livestream on my computer.
  2. When it detects an exciting moment, it cuts a clip and adds captions.
  3. Using TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API, it uploads the clip to the TikTok account I have authorized.

The tool is operated only by its owner and only posts to the owner’s own account.
